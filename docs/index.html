GAP-Bench

An open benchmark for AI that protects users when expert help is out of reach.

Existing safety benchmarks measure whether a model refuses harmful requests. GAP-Bench measures something else: whether a model recognises a hidden risk in the user's situation and takes the protective action.

acceptance criterion

A scenario is added if it would protect a vulnerable user who cannot easily reach a qualified expert when something matters.

Closed, paid AI doesn't reach them well. Free, open-weight models running on a phone or laptop are the most scalable option that does. The open question is whether they actually protect users today.

failure modes

Source-referenced ground truth from public authorities (regulators, medical guidelines, official law texts). 222 scenarios carry real risk; 78 are control scenarios with no risk — together they make the score ungameable by always-warn or always-reassure strategies.

Where it goes next.

v0.2 scales the dataset under the same acceptance criterion (more domains, more sources — not necessarily more languages), runs a fine-tuning experiment on Gemma 4 to test whether the dataset functions as a training target, and adds a second-judge calibration so readers can gauge how much of the v0.1 result depends on the choice of judge.
