Key findings · v0.1

Only from the actual run: Gemma 4 E2B + E4B on 300 scenarios judged by gemini-3-flash-preview. Closed-model baselines, 26B, and fine-tuned variants not yet tested.

Finding 01 · size lift

Gemma 4 E4B (10 GB) scores 84.1 · E2B (3 GB) scores 67.0. The bigger size is worth +17 protection points on the same 300 scenarios, free and local. Both fit on consumer hardware.

Finding 02 · the gap is over-caution

Most of the +17 gap comes from staying calm on safe questions (+20.5 points: 56.4 → 76.9), not from catching more risks (+10.4: 82.4 → 92.8). E2B over-warns on safe questions about 44% of the time; E4B does it 23%. Over-cautious refusal is the dominant remaining failure mode.

Finding 03 · per-domain pattern

E4B wins every domain. Scam detection is nearly saturated (99.0). The two domains with the largest lift are bureaucracy (+14) and control (+12.5) — exactly the two where E2B was weakest.

Caveats · v0.1

n = 300; Wilson 95% CI is wide. Single judge (gemini-3-flash-preview), no inter-judge agreement check yet. No closed-model baselines yet — claims that compare Gemma to GPT or Claude await those runs. No fine-tuning experiments yet; lift over base Gemma is TBD.
